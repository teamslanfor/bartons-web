Questions

Delivery FAQs

When will my order be delivered?

Choose your delivery date at checkout. Next-day delivery is available for local orders placed before 3pm. We can’t guarantee a time, and orders are delivered between 9am and 5pm.

How much is home delivery?

Local home delivery is £5.95, with a minimum order spend of £50.

Where will my order be left?

Our drivers leave your order at your door. If you have special instructions, add them at checkout and we’ll follow them where possible.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Click & collect from our Hutton shop is available for orders of any value. Choose a half-hour slot during shop opening hours. Same-day collections aren’t available.

How do I pay?

Send your order through the website and we’ll call you to confirm it and take payment by card. You can also order by phone on 01772 971601.
