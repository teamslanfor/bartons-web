Privacy policy

What we collect

When you place an order or send a message we collect the details you give us: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and order details.

How we use it

We use your details to process and deliver your order, to contact you about it and to take payment by phone. We don’t sell your information.

Your basket

Your basket is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it’s still there when you come back. It isn’t sent to us until you place your order.

Service providers

Order and contact forms are delivered to our email through FormSubmit. The embedded Google Map and Google Fonts may set their own cookies.

Your rights

To access, correct or delete your information, email orders@bartonsbakehouse.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
